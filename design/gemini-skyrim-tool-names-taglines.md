Here is a refined, player-accessible set of Skyrim-themed names and taglines for your **Vortex Collection Tools** UI. Every option leverages high-recognition in-game concepts, mapping their true in-world mechanics directly to what your toolset accomplishes behind the scenes.

## **1\. Home / Launcher**

*Landing page showing all tools as cards—the primary hub where your journey starts.*

### **Name Options**

* **The Arcaneum** — Skyrim’s legendary library at the College of Winterhold; the central repository where every piece of knowledge and tool is cataloged.  
* **The Hall of Attainment** — The main living quarters and hub for mages in Winterhold; where you gather your tools before venturing out.  
* **The Dragonborn’s Sanctum** — Evokes a home base (like Breezehome or High Hrothgar) where all your gear and preparations are laid out in one place.

### **Themed Tagline**

*"Your central sanctuary for every tool, spell, and scroll needed to master your collection."*

## **2\. Rebuild Collection**

*Re-extracts an installed collection’s mods and FOMOD choices directly and fast to repair corrupted files.*

### **Name Options**

* **Restoration** — The Skyrim school of magic dedicated entirely to mending damage and restoring what was broken back to full health.  
* **Fast Travel** — The classic game mechanic for bypassing long, tedious journeys—here bypassing Vortex’s sluggish reinstall process.  
* **The Amulet of Arkay** — Named for the God of Birth and Death, symbolic of bringing a broken or dead mod list back to life instantly.

### **Themed Tagline**

*"Mends broken and corrupted files in seconds, skipping the slow journey of a full reinstallation."*

## **3\. Update Collection**

*Preserves your custom "Ignored" or "Disabled" choices through a collection update and reapplies them.*

### **Name Options**

* **The Ward** — Protective magic that blocks unwanted interference; protects your carefully ignored/disabled settings from being wiped out by an update.  
* **Unrelenting Force** — The iconic Shout (*Fus Ro Dah*) that holds your ground and stops the incoming update from pushing your preferences around.  
* **Spellbreaker** — Skyrim’s legendary shield that wards off incoming spell effects; shields your personal mod state against disruptive update overwrites.

### **Themed Tagline**

*"Shields your personal ignored and disabled choices so updates can’t wipe out your custom setup."*

## **4\. Rules Generator**

*Copies custom load-order conflict rules from an older version of a collection to the newly updated version.*

### **Name Options**

* **The Scribe** — Scholars across Skyrim who transcribe ancient knowledge from old scrolls into new volumes without losing a word.  
* **Transmute** — The alteration spell that takes existing raw metal and turns it into pure gold; turns your old conflict resolutions into ready-to-use new rules.  
* **The Way of the Voice** — The ancient tradition passed down across generations without changing the core rules of the Way.

### **Themed Tagline**

*"Transcribes your proven load-order rules from your old collection straight into the update."*

## **5\. Merge Plugins**

*Merges multiple plugins into a single file (flagged ESL when eligible) to free up load-order slots.*

### **Name Options**

* **The Forge** — Where blacksmiths heat separate pieces of iron and steel and hammer them into a single, cohesive piece of armor or weaponry.  
* **The Aetherium Forge** — The legendary underground forge capable of combining rare, disparate materials into one powerful artifact.  
* **Alchemy** — The skill of crushing individual ingredients together in a mortar and pestle to produce a single, potent elixir.

### **Themed Tagline**

*"Fuses separate plugins into a single, compact file to keep you well clear of the plugin limit."*

## **6\. Missing Masters**

*Detects missing required master plugins (preventing CTDs on boot) and guides you to fix them.*

### **Name Options**

* **The Augur** — Inspired by the Augur of Dunlain; a entity of pure vision that reveals hidden dangers before they destroy you.  
* **Detect Life** — The alteration spell that highlights unseen threats lurking right around the corner before they strike.  
* **Clairvoyance** — The spell that draws a glowing path directly toward what you need, guiding you straight to missing requirements.

### **Themed Tagline**

*"Reveals missing prerequisite files before you launch, sparing you from a sudden crash to desktop."*

## **7\. Vortex Scrub**

*Scans for and cleans out leftover staging folders and orphaned downloaded archives no longer tracked by Vortex.*

### **Name Options**

* **The Cleansing** — Directly recalls *The Cleansing of the Stones* questline; purifying corrupt remnants left behind in the environment.  
* **Cleanse Light** / **Sun Fire** — Restoration spells designed specifically to turn junk and corrupted remnants into ash.  
* **Banish** — The conjuration spell that sends unwanted, lingering entities back to the void where they belong.

### **Themed Tagline**

*"Banishes orphaned downloads and abandoned staging folders back to the void to reclaim disk space."*

## **8\. Archive Finder**

*Indexes every archive in your downloads folder so you can search inside all of them instantly without unpacking.*

### **Name Options**

* **Clairvoyance** — The iconic spell that reveals hidden paths and locates exact items tucked away out of sight.  
* **Detect Dead** / **Detect Object** — Spells that scan through solid stone and walls to reveal what’s hidden inside containers.  
* **The Word Wall** — Ancient monuments that let you read deep, hidden knowledge carved into stone without digging beneath the surface.

### **Themed Tagline**

*"Sees straight through packed archives to locate exact files without unpacking a single byte."*

## **9\. Reports (Section & Sub-Reports)**

*System dashboards that track history, problem mods, diffs, and rule statuses.*

├── 9\. Reports (Parent Section)  
│   ├── 9a. Stats  
│   ├── 9b. Work Through  
│   ├── 9c. Update Compare  
│   └── 9d. Rules Generator Report

### **9\. Reports (Parent)**

* **The Chronicle** — The grand historical record keeping track of all events across the realm.  
* **The Great Library** — The collection of all records, tallies, and knowledge in Winterhold.  
* **The Elder Records** — A timeless ledger detailing every state of your collection.  
* **Tagline:** *"Your complete library of collection health, historical stats, and update ledgers."*

### **9a. Stats**

* **The Ledger** — The classic merchant’s book recording tallies, counts, and performance metrics.  
* **The Scoreboard** — Clear, instant recognition of performance tallies and build counts.  
* **Level Up** — Celebrates tracking your growing statistics and successful build runs over time.  
* **Tagline:** *"Tracks your total rebuild runs, file counts, and processing speeds over time."*

### **9b. Work Through**

* **The Quest Log** — Skyrim’s universally recognized list of active objectives and remaining tasks to complete.  
* **Bounty Board** — The list of troublesome targets left to hunt down and resolve across the holds.  
* **Active Objectives** — Direct in-game UI terminology for tasks that still need your immediate attention.  
* **Tagline:** *"Your active checklist of unresolved mod conflicts and problem files left to fix."*

### **9c. Update Compare**

* **Scroll of Retrospection** — An Elder Scroll that peers backward in time, holding the past state side-by-side with the present.  
* **The Dragonbreak** — Named after the lore event where two alternate timelines exist at once until compared and reconciled.  
* **Mirror of Galathil** — Named after the Face Sculptor in the Ragged Flagon who alters your look, showing what you were versus what you've become.  
* **Tagline:** *"Compares your previous setup against the new update to show exactly what was added, kept, or removed."*

### **9d. Rules Generator Report**

* **The Scribe’s Ledger** — The detailed audit log created by The Scribe showing transcribed work.  
* **Transmutation Audit** — A detailed breakdown of which raw rules were successfully converted and which need manual forging.  
* **The Court Scholar’s Notes** — The precise breakdown of which rules applied cleanly and which require closer inspection.  
* **Tagline:** *"Details which conflict rules were successfully transferred and flags any that need manual review."*

## **10\. Settings**

*Configure your game paths, staging directories, and tool preferences once for all tools.*

### **Name Options**

* **The Standing Stones** — The ancient guardians across Skyrim where you choose your permanent foundational blessings and paths.  
* **The Serpent Stone** — Symbolic of foundational, persistent rules that govern how everything else behaves.  
* **The Calibration** — Plain yet thematic option evoking Dwemer levers and dials set to align the system.

### **Themed Tagline**

*"Set your foundational paths and preferences once to align every tool in the arsenal."*
