⚠️ Before fetching, read this

        🔄 Update Without Redoing Your Mod Cleanup

        Updating a collection in Vortex often resets your carefully-set Ignored and Disabled mods back to their defaults. Work through the steps below in order — you'll hop over to Vortex between some of them, and your place here is saved while you do.

        ⚠️ Quick heads-up before you start!
        • Close Vortex first: Make sure Vortex is completely closed before you proceed so your updates save cleanly.
        • Mark your mods first: Make sure any mod you want to keep is already marked as Ignored or Disabled in Vortex. This tool only saves what's currently set—it won't change statuses for you!
        • Delete unwanted mods: If you marked a mod as Ignored to get rid of it, be sure to actually remove (delete) it in Vortex too. Setting a mod to Ignored just stops Vortex from reinstalling it later, but won't disable or remove an active mod.

                            🧩 Create a Dummy Master?

                            🧩 Understanding Dummy Master Files

                            A dummy master is a lightweight placeholder file (.esm, .esp, or .esl) created solely to trick Skyrim into thinking a required dependency is installed. This prevents the game from immediately crashing on startup when a master file is missing.

                            💡 When to use a dummy master

                            • Bypassing requirements: Useful if a patch or mod demands a master plugin you don't actually need or want in your setup.
                            • Merged mods: Helps older patches recognize merged plugins that replaced original .esp files.
                            ⚠️ Important Caveat: Resolving Conflicts

                            Generating a dummy master prevents startup crashes, but it doesn't add actual mod data. If the dependent mod relies on scripts, textures, or specific game records from that missing master, you may still run into in-game glitches or missing content. After creating a dummy master, you might still need to manually resolve conflicts or adjust your load order in Vortex to ensure everything runs smoothly.
