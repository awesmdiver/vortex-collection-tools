🧰 Your Whole Vortex Toolkit, in One Place

Pick a tool below to get started. Everything here works directly with your installed Vortex collections—rebuild them faster, protect your Ignored and Disabled mods through updates, untangle rule conflicts, and review your progress.

⚠️ Don't change things in Vortex while this tool is working

Some tools here (like applying an update or fixing a rule cycle) write changes directly to Vortex in real time. If you also click something like Deploy Mods inside Vortex at the exact same moment, both programs will try to edit your files at once—which can corrupt your load order.

The golden rule: let this tool finish completely before clicking anything inside Vortex, and vice versa.

                                      ℹ️ What is a dummy master?

                                      A dummy master is a lightweight placeholder file (.esm, .esp, or .esl) that tricks Skyrim into thinking a required mod is installed.

                                      When to use one:

                                      • Skipping unwanted requirements: Useful when a patch requires a master file you don't want or need in your setup.
                                      • Merged mods: Helps older patches recognize plugins that were merged into a different file.
                                      ⚠️ Dummy masters do not restore missing content

                                      A dummy master only stops startup crashes. If other mods depend on scripts, textures, or records from the missing file, you may still run into in-game glitches or broken quests.
